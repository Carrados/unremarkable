Design fiction
Unremarkable is written as if it were a business. It isn't taking work yet.
What that means

Nothing was kept. Here is what would happen next.

Unremarkable isn't taking work yet, so what you wrote was checked and thrown away. If it were trading, this is what would happen:

  1. Within one working day, an email from a person, not a system, asking when suits you for a conversation.
  2. A conversation of about an hour, by phone or video, about what people come to you to do.
  3. Within a week, a written proposal: what we would build, the price and when it would be live.
