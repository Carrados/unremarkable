Websites that work for the people who use them, and for the agents they send.

People come to your website to do something: find an answer, choose a service, book a place. Increasingly, they send an agent to do it for them. We design, build and look after websites for both.

## The rule

Unremarkable has one rule, and it overrides every other: **it never is.**

Every site we make has to impress everyone who meets it, whether they see it, hear it through a screen reader, feel it in braille or send an agent to fetch it for them. A site that dazzles the eye and stumbles in a screen reader isn't impressive. It's half finished.

## How we build

- **Built for people who hear the web.** Every site is tested with a screen reader by someone who uses one every day, not run through a checker and declared done.
- **Built for the agents people send.** Every page has a Markdown version, and every site has an llms.txt, so an AI agent sent by a person gets what that person would, from the menu to the footer.
- **Built to a standard.** Our sites are built to [Zero Information Casualty v1.7](https://zeroinformationcasualty.org/): nothing published is lost on its way to any reader.
- **No plugins to patch.** Plain pages served fast from the edge: no plugins to update, no database to break into, and not a line of JavaScript needed to read a word.
- **Yours, all of it.** The site, its code, the hosting and the domain sit in your accounts. We work inside them, and you can show us out.
- **A website that listens.** No editor to learn. You say what you want, see it on your own site, and publish it, or change it again.

## Our work

These are our own sites: working publications where we develop and use the approach we offer here. We depend on them ourselves, and what we build gets used on them first.

1. [**blind-ant.com**](https://blind-ant.com/): A making practice that publishes often: design fiction, stories, games and apps. Its pieces are published by our agent from an in-tray, twice a day, and the worked example is one of its jobs.
2. [**carrados.com**](https://carrados.com/): Writing about AI and blindness that has to be accountable for its evidence: the frameworks, the Papers and The Blind AI, with a desk and an address of its own for each of its writers.
3. [**charli-jo.com**](https://charli-jo.com/): A practice in accessible service design for people and the agents they send. Its principle and method began as Word documents and are now kept as the pages themselves.
4. [**charli.info**](https://charli.info/): The front door of the CharliVerse, a shared fictional world, and CharliPedia, its memory: an encyclopaedia written for people and for agents, which any AI agent can also look things up in through a connector of its own.
5. [**Zero Information Casualty**](https://zeroinformationcasualty.org/): The web standard every one of these sites is built to, published on a site built to it.
