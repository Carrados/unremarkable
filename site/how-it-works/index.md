You tell us what people come to you to do. We build the website that lets them do it.

## From first conversation to live

1. **A conversation.** What you offer, who comes to you and what they are trying to get done: book a place, find an answer, choose a service. That understanding shapes everything else.
2. **Your accounts, in your name.** We help you open a Cloudflare account for hosting and a GitHub organisation for the code, and register the domain to you. You add us as a member of each. For email and files we recommend Google Workspace and set it up for you, including the records that keep your email out of spam. If you already use Microsoft 365, that works too.
3. **Design and build.** A design made for you, not a theme. You see each version on a private preview address, and you can listen to it as well as look at it.
4. **Tested by ear.** Before it goes live, the site is tested with a screen reader by someone who uses one every day, and it gets an accessibility statement that says what was checked and when.
5. **Live, and handed over.** The site goes live with a short handover note: how it is published, where reports go, and how to remove us as a member if you ever want to.

## After launch: tell it what you want

You don't edit your website. You tell it what you want, and it's done.

Every page of your site has a link only you and your team use, "Change something on this page". Write what you want in your own words: the opening hours are wrong, add the June event, say more about the new service. We make the change and email you a link to see it. Reply to approve it, and it goes live.

Visitors get their own link, "Fix something on this page", for anything that stops them, whether they read by eye, by screen reader, by braille or by agent. A person reads every report.

Things that change often, such as events, news and opening times, can live in a Google Sheet that your site reads when it is published. You edit something you already know how to use.

## You own everything

The site, its source code, the hosting account and the domain are all yours, and you pay the running costs directly. We work inside your accounts as a member. There is no platform to be locked into: the site is plain HTML and a short build script, so any developer, or any agent you send, can pick it up.

## Nothing to patch

Most of the cost of a website built on a platform comes after launch: plugin updates, security patches and cleaning up after break-ins. Our sites are plain pages served from the edge, with no plugins and no database behind them, so there is nothing to patch and very little that can break.

## What we don't build

Online shops. Shopify and WooCommerce are good at them. We build sites for organisations whose visitors come to find out, choose, book and ask: charities, practices, venues, clubs and small firms.
