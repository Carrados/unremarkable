You tell us what people come to you to do. We build the website that lets them do it.

## From first conversation to live

1. **A conversation.** What you offer, who comes to you and what they are trying to get done: book a place, find an answer, choose a service. That understanding shapes everything else.
2. **Your accounts, in your name.** We help you open a Cloudflare account for hosting and a GitHub organisation for the code, and register the domain to you. You add us as a member of each. For email and files we recommend Google Workspace and set it up for you, including the records that keep your email out of spam. If you already use Microsoft 365, that works too.
3. **Design and build.** A design made for you, not a theme. You see each version on a private preview address, and you can listen to it as well as look at it.
4. **Tested by ear.** Before it goes live, every kind of page in the design is tested with a screen reader by someone who uses one every day, and the site gets an accessibility statement that says what was checked and when.
5. **Live, and handed over.** The site goes live with a handover note: how it is published, where reports go, how to remove us as a member, and instructions another developer or an AI agent could follow to look after it without us.

## Intention-based publishing

Tell us what you want. We prepare it on a private copy of your site. You decide what goes live.

There is no editor and no content management system to learn. Your website is changed the way you would ask a person to change it.

1. **Say what you want.** Put it in your in-tray: a shared folder in your own Google Drive, or one email address. Write it in your own words, or drop in what you already have, a flyer, a document, the photos from the event: the opening hours are wrong, add the June event, put this on the services page.
2. **See it on your own site.** We make the change on a private copy of your site and send you a link to it. Open it on your own phone or computer, with your own screen reader or magnifier if you use one: the way your visitors will meet it, not a preview in somebody else's tool.
3. **Publish, or change it again.** If it's right, publish it, and it's live. If it isn't, say what's still wrong, and it goes round again. Nothing reaches your visitors until you say so.

Cloud Dancer, our publishing agent, built on Claude, turns what you send into a proposed change. We build and run that process, handle anything that goes wrong, and stand behind every change it makes.

Every change is made within the design you agreed, and that design has already been heard. Automated checks run on every change, and the receipt sets out anything Cloud Dancer wrote itself, such as a picture's description, so you can check it. A new kind of page is heard before it goes live, and the whole design is heard again every month.

No editor also means nothing for a screen reader to stumble over. If you can write an email, you can run your website.

Things that change often, such as events, news and opening times, can live in a Google Sheet that your site reads when it is published. You edit something you already know how to use.

Fixing works the same way. Visitors get their own link, "Fix something on this page", for anything that stops them, whether they read by eye, by screen reader, by braille or by agent. The fix is made on a private copy and tested the way the problem was described, and when it's right, you publish it. Nobody gives an email address: whoever reported it gets a link to their report's own page, which tells them when the fix is live and asks whether it works for them now. If it doesn't, it goes round again. Every report is read first by Phix (said "fix"), our fixing agent, built on Claude. It finds the problem, makes the fix on a private copy and sends it to you. You publish it or send it back with your reasons. Nothing Phix makes goes live without you, and every fix leaves a test behind, so the same problem can't quietly come back.

## You own everything

The site, its source code, the hosting account and the domain are all yours, and you pay the running costs directly. We work inside your accounts as a member. Your pages are plain HTML and a short build script in your own repository, so any developer, or any agent you send, can pick them up. The forms run on Cloudflare, and would need adapting to move them anywhere else.

[Questions about your accounts and what they cost](https://unremarkable.me/questions/)

## No plugins to patch

Most of the cost of a website built on a platform comes after launch: plugin updates, security patches and cleaning up after break-ins. Our sites are plain pages served from the edge, with no plugins and no database behind them, so there are no plugins to patch and very little that can break. What little code there is, the build script and the forms, we keep up to date as part of looking after your site.

## What we don't build

Online shops. Shopify and WooCommerce are good at them. We build sites for organisations whose visitors come to find out, choose, book and ask: charities, practices, venues, clubs and small firms.
