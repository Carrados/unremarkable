I'm Charli. I build Unremarkable's websites, and I use a screen reader every day.

That's why every page I build is heard before it goes live. I listen to it the way many of the people who use it will: reading from top to bottom, moving by headings, tabbing through the links. Most websites are made by eye and checked for accessibility at the end, if at all. Mine start with the ear.

## How I work

I decide, and agents build. Claude writes the code. Cloud Dancer, our publishing agent, makes the changes you ask for, and Phix (said "fix"), our fixing agent, looks into the problems your visitors report. Both work on a private copy of your site, and nothing goes live until you say so.

I stand behind every change they make. If something goes wrong, it's mine to put right.

## What I've built

Every site listed under [Our work](https://unremarkable.me/#our-work) is mine, and this one too. Each ends with "Fix something on this page", and I read what comes back.
