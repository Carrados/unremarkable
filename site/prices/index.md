One price to build your site, and one monthly price to look after it.

## The build

- **Price:** from £3,500, paid in two halves: one when we start and one when the site goes live
- **Includes:** the conversation, the design, the build, setting up your accounts and email records, testing with a screen reader, the accessibility statement and the handover note
- **Takes:** four to six weeks for most sites

The price depends on how many kinds of page your site needs, not how many pages it has. A site with a hundred events costs no more than a site with ten.

## Looking after it

- **Price:** £75 a month, cancel with a month's notice
- **Includes:** changes to your content and pages within the agreed design, sent to your in-tray; every visitor report from "Fix something on this page"; keeping the build script, forms and agent files up to date; and the whole design heard again by ear each month
- **Turnaround:** content changes made within two working days, and work started within one working day on anything that stops a visitor
- **Not included:** new features, such as a booking system, and redesigns, which are priced before any work starts

Most fixes are made the same day. Some causes sit outside the site, with a browser, a screen reader or a provider, and then we tell you what we found and what we are doing about it.

## If you leave

You own the website. The monthly price pays for the service that changes and looks after it. So when you stop:

- Your site stays live. It is in your accounts, and it doesn't need us to stay up.
- We stop watching your in-tray, and changes stop being made for you.
- You keep the complete repository, with written instructions that another developer, or an AI agent you trust, can follow to change and publish it.
- We take our "Fix something on this page" link off your pages, or hand its reports over to whoever looks after the site next.
- We remove ourselves as members of your accounts, and tell you when it's done.

## Running costs, paid by you

- **Domain:** usually £10 to £40 a year, depending on the ending
- **Hosting:** nothing for most sites on Cloudflare's free plan, because visits to plain pages aren't charged; if your forms get very busy, Cloudflare's paid plan is from US$5 a month, plus usage above what it includes, and we tell you before you need it
- **Email and files:** Google Workspace is charged per person per month, and is free for eligible charities through Google for Nonprofits

You pay these to the providers directly. We never mark them up, because we never hold them.
