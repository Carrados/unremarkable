Websites that work for the people who use them, and for the agents they send.

People come to your website to do something: find an answer, choose a service, book a place. Increasingly, they send an agent to do it for them. We design, build and look after websites for both.

The rule

Unremarkable has one rule, and it overrides every other: it never is.

Every site we make has to impress everyone who meets it, whether they see it, hear it through a screen reader, feel it in braille or send an agent to fetch it for them. A site that dazzles the eye and stumbles in a screen reader isn't impressive. It's half finished.

How we build

  • Built for people who hear the web. Every site is tested with a screen reader by someone who uses one every day, not run through a checker and declared done.
  • Built for the agents people send. Every page has a Markdown version, and every site has an llms.txt, so an AI agent sent by a person gets what that person would, from the menu to the footer.
  • Built to a standard. Our sites are built to Zero Information Casualty v1.7: nothing published is lost on its way to any reader.
  • No plugins to patch. Plain pages served fast from the edge: no plugins to update, no database to break into, and not a line of JavaScript needed to read a word.
  • Yours, all of it. The site, its code, the hosting and the domain sit in your accounts. We work inside them, and you can show us out.
  • A website that listens. No editor to learn. You say what you want, see it on your own site, and publish it, or change it again.
