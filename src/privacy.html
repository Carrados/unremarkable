Only one form on this site keeps anything: "Fix something on this page". This is what it keeps, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

What is kept

What you write in your report, the address of the page it is about, how you were reading the page if you tell us, and your browser and device as your browser reports them. We don't ask for your name or your email address, and we don't keep your IP address: it is used for a moment to stop the form being flooded, then forgotten.

Your report is read by Phix (said "fix"), our fixing agent, built on Claude, and by the person who looks after the site it is about. Anyone who has your report's link can see it, because the link is the only key, so keep it to yourself.

How long it is kept

Twelve months from the day you send it. Then the report, and everything that happened to it, is deleted automatically, and its link stops working.

Having a report deleted sooner

Email hey@unremarkable.me with your report's link, and it will be deleted. The link is how we know the report is yours, so you needn't tell us who you are.

The Start form

The Start form keeps nothing while Unremarkable is design fiction. What you write is checked and thrown away. When that changes, this page will say exactly what it keeps before you send anything.

Who is responsible

Charli-Jo Tyrer runs Unremarkable and answers for what this site keeps. Questions go to the same address, hey@unremarkable.me.
